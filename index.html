Privacy Policy – My Apps

Last updated: May 10, 2026

Thank you for using my apps! This privacy policy applies to all mobile applications created by Jayesh Dhanesha. I take your privacy very seriously and collect only the minimum data needed for each app to work properly.

1. General Principle

I do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, physical address, or contacts. I do not "link" the technical data I collect to your real-world identity.

2. Data Collected for Functionality & Analytics

To ensure my apps are reliable and to provide the features you purchased, the following data is collected (Not Linked to Your Identity):

  • Identifiers: Anonymous User IDs and Device IDs are used to manage subscription access and prevent fraud.
  • Purchase History: We track which premium features you have unlocked to ensure they are available across your devices.
  • Diagnostics: Crash logs and performance metrics (e.g., download speeds) help fix bugs and optimize the app for iOS.
  • Anonymous Analytics: General usage patterns (e.g., button taps) help me improve the app interface.

3. App-Specific Data Collection

Certain apps may require specific hardware access to function:

  • Location Services: If an app provides map or location-based tools, precise location is processed locally on your device to show relevant info. We do not store your location history on our servers.
  • Local Network: Used only in apps that require interaction with local files or nearby devices (like Minecraft world importing).

4. Advertisements (AdMob)

Some apps display ads. These are provided by Google AdMob:

  • AdMob may collect Advertising IDs (IDFA) to show relevant ads.
  • You can opt-out of personalized tracking via the "App Tracking Transparency" prompt on iOS.
  • Full details: Google Privacy Policy

5. Subscriptions & In-App Purchases

For apps offering premium features via subscription:

  • Payment Processing: All payments are handled securely by Apple. I never see or store your credit card or bank details.
  • Subscription Management: I use RevenueCat to verify your purchase status. This uses an anonymous ID to confirm if your subscription is active.
  • Restore Purchases: You can always restore your purchases on any device linked to your Apple ID.
Subscription Terms (Auto-Renewal):
Subscriptions automatically renew unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24-hours before the end of the current period. Your iTunes Account will be charged for renewal within 24-hours prior to the end of the current period. You can manage or turn off auto-renew in your Apple ID Account Settings at any time after purchase.

6. Third-Party Services

I use trusted third-party services to help run my apps:

  • Google Firebase & AdMob (Analytics & Ads)
  • RevenueCat (Subscription Logic)

7. Children’s Privacy

My apps are not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If any such data is found, it is deleted immediately.
